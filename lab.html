// about

A digital playground operated by one person, from a phone, for reasons that made sense at the time and have not been reviewed since.

Contains experiments, games, tools, traps, generators, galleries, machines, and junk. Some of it works. Most of it barely does. All of it exists on purpose.

No roadmap. No investors. No adults supervising. Just curiosity and a phone with a reasonable data plan.
